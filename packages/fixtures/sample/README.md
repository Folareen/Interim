# Sample personas

Two synthetic people, each in five hair stages: 0 full, 1 thinner, 2 short, 3 none, 4 growing back. They are not real people. They are the basis of the demo's sample profiles, which the app labels as simulated (docs/product/overview.md, Demo plan).

Made on 2026-10-21 with YouCam's AI Image Generator V2 (`youcam-image-v2`), then resized to 1000 px high with metadata removed. Stage 0 came from text to image, and each later stage from image to image on stage 0, so the face, clothes and background stay the same.

Stage 0, man: "Candid smartphone selfie photo of a 42 year old man with full, dark brown, short to medium length hair, looking straight at the camera, relaxed slight smile, wearing a plain grey crew-neck t-shirt, even soft daylight, plain light wall background, head and shoulders, realistic skin texture, ordinary phone camera quality."

Stage 0, woman: the same, for "a 38 year old woman with long, dark brown, straight hair past her shoulders" in "a plain navy crew-neck top".

Stages 1 to 4 are edits that start "Keep the exact same person: the same face, skin, expression, clothing, lighting and plain background. Change only the hair." and then describe the hair: noticeably thinner with a receding hairline or a widening centre parting, cut very short (a buzz cut, or a pixie cut), completely bald with a smooth scalp, or a very short even layer of dark hair growing back.

These are inputs. Recorded YouCam responses for them are made by running the pipeline in record mode, since a fixture is named by the pipeline's cache key.
